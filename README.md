# Primetrade.ai Data Science Intern Assignment

## Part C - Actionable output

Based on the analysis, here are 2 rules of thumb:

1. **During Fear days, reduce leverage limits for unprofitable traders.** The data shows these accounts consistently try to buy the dip during bear markets and end up losing more money. Limiting their long exposure protects both the traders and the platform.

2. **During Greed days, increase trade frequency limits and leverage for consistent winners.** Their win rates naturally peak when sentiment is bullish, so allowing them to take larger or more frequent positions helps maximize overall upside capture.
